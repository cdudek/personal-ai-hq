# Adapter: sales and customer success

## Tool defaults
CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce or Pipedrive), mail and calendar, chat (Slack or Teams), meeting recordings (Gong, Fireflies, Granola or Demodesk), docs and proposals (Google Docs or Slides), support tickets (Zendesk, Intercom or HubSpot).

## Routine emphasis
- `radar`: mail and CRM tasks first. Replies owed to prospects and customers, deals with no activity in 7 days, renewals due in 30 days. Skip the code section.
- `brief`: today's calls with account context (stage, last contact, open questions).
- `meeting-prep`: customer calls get a topic block built from the CRM record: stage, value, last notes, open objections.
- `weekly`: pipeline changes, renewals and churn risks as the dashboard block; recurring inputs such as a weekly metrics post.
- `review`: learn the style of outreach, follow-ups and renewal mails per language.

## Glossary seeds
- **ARR / MRR**: annual / monthly recurring revenue
- **NRR**: net revenue retention
- **MQL / SQL**: marketing / sales qualified lead
- **Champion**: the person at the customer who pushes your case
- **QBR**: quarterly business review
- **Churn**: a customer leaving

## Typical meeting types
Discovery call, demo, proposal review, negotiation, onboarding kickoff, QBR, renewal call, pipeline review, handover from sales to success.

## Default areas (setup proposes, you confirm)
- **Pipeline:** new deals. Prospects, demos, proposals, negotiation.
- **Accounts:** existing customers. Onboarding, renewals, QBRs, escalations, churn risks.
- **Team:** people, if you lead any. Hiring, 1:1s, coaching.
- **Company:** up and across. Management, forecast, marketing, product feedback.
- **Admin:** contracts, expenses, CRM hygiene, access.
- **Me:** own growth and outside presence.

Example tie-break: 1 about a specific person on your team: Team. 2 about a named customer under contract: Accounts. 3 about a prospect or a new deal: Pipeline. 4 for someone inside the company: Company. 5 pure bureaucracy: Admin. 6 for yourself: Me.
