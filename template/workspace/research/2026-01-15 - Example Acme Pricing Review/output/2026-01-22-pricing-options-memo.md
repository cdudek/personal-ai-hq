# Pricing options for the Starter plan

Recommendation: raise to 24 per seat for new sign-ups only, review churn after 60 days.

1. Keep 19: no risk, no revenue change.
2. Raise to 24 for new sign-ups: small risk, about 20 percent more revenue per new seat.
3. Raise to 24 for everyone: highest revenue, highest churn risk.
