# Board

Claude maintains this file. You read it in the daily and weekly views and say "done", "move" or "drop" in chat. Every task has a `closed when:` line and a source.

Format: `- [ ] <task> · id: <short-slug, unique, never reused> · P0-P3 · size: XS|S|M|L · domain: <...> · status: <Not started|In progress|Waiting> · closed when: <...> · source: <chat|mail|tickets|code|meeting|voice|you|...> · link: <url> · added: YYYY-MM-DD`

Notes, context quotes and history go as indented `  - ` lines under the task. The views show them in the task's detail panel.
